About The Knight Post

The Knight Post is an independent blog and local directory built for Rutgers–New Brunswick students. We cover food, study spots, deals, and campus life, and we maintain a directory of local businesses students actually use.

What we're not

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Rutgers University. "Rutgers" and related marks belong to the university; we use them descriptively to say who we're for.

How the directory works

Listings are built from public business information. Businesses can claim or upgrade their listing — see our advertise page for details. If you spot something outdated, let us know.
