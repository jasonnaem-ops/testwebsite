The student discounts nobody tells you about

By The Knight Post Staff · 3 min read

Some of the best discounts near campus aren't advertised — you just have to ask, or flash a RUID. Here's what to look out for.

Ask before you order

Several restaurants near College Ave offer a quiet 10% student discount that isn't posted on a menu or sign. It costs nothing to ask when you're paying.

Off-peak timing helps

Some spots run informal deals during slow hours — mid-afternoon on weekdays is usually your best shot at a deal that isn't listed anywhere official.

Keep your RUID on you

Beyond the university's own partner discounts, some independent local businesses will knock off a percentage just for showing a valid student ID, even if it's not something they promote.

We're building out a running list of confirmed discounts in the directory — if you know of one we're missing, let us know on the about page.
