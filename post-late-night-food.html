Where to eat on College Ave after midnight

By The Knight Post Staff · 5 min read

Finals week, a bad exam, or just a Tuesday that got away from you — at some point every Rutgers student ends up on College Ave at 1am looking for food. Here's what's actually worth the walk.

Hansel 'n Griddle

Open around the clock, this diner has been a fixture on Easton Ave for longer than most of us have been alive. Go for disco fries if you've never had them; go for the omelets if it's technically breakfast time for you and no one else.

Stuff Yer Face

Known for stromboli, but the late-night menu covers more ground than that. Expect a wait on weekends after 11pm — it's popular for a reason.

RU Hungry Grill

Cash only, but fast and cheap, which matters more at 1am than a card reader does. A reliable option when the diners have a line out the door.

A note on hours

Hours shift during breaks and summer session, so double check before you make the walk. We keep the directory updated with current info where we can.
