Five places to study that aren't Alexander Library

By The Knight Post Staff · 4 min read

Alexander Library during midterms is a battlefield for outlets and seats. Here's where else to go when it's full — or when you just need a change of scenery.

Wonder Bar Coffee

A quieter independent shop downtown. Good for focused work rather than group study — there isn't a ton of table space, but the noise level is low.

Panera on Easton Ave

Reliable wifi, plenty of outlets, and it's open late. It gets crowded around dinner time, so aim for off-peak hours if you want a table to yourself.

Busch Campus Center

If you're on the Busch side for engineering or science classes, the campus center has quiet corners that don't get the same foot traffic as the library.

  • Best for groups: Panera, Busch Campus Center
  • Best for silence: Wonder Bar Coffee
  • Best for late nights: Alexander Library Cafe (extended finals hours)

Check current hours and vibe for each in the directory before you make the trip.
