List your business

The Knight Post directory is where Rutgers students check before deciding where to eat, study, or spend a weekend. Here's how to get in front of them.

Free listing

Basic listings — name, category, address, and hours — are free. Email us your business details and we'll add you.

Featured listing

Featured businesses appear at the top of relevant searches with a highlighted border and a "Featured" tag. This is the best option if you want consistent visibility, especially during the school year when search traffic peaks around move-in, midterms, and finals.

Blog mentions

Businesses featured in roundup posts (like "Where to eat after midnight") tend to see a traffic bump when those posts rank well or get shared. We write these independently, but businesses can reach out if there's a good story to tell.
